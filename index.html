Common questions

Before you start

Can SizeProof guarantee every target size?

No. Some page sets cannot remain readable under an extremely small limit. SizeProof reports that honestly instead of claiming a false success.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Photo-to-PDF processing happens on your device. Read the privacy policy for optional analytics and rewarded-ad details.
