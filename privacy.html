Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

SizeProof processes the photos you select and the PDFs it creates on your device. The app does not upload document content to a SizeProof server, require an account, include advertising SDKs, or collect analytics.

Information used by the app

  • Selected photos: copied temporarily so the app can order, rotate, compress, and render them.
  • Generated PDFs: retained temporarily so you can preview, share, or save them.
  • Purchase state: Apple supplies verified StoreKit entitlement information. The app caches whether a verified lifetime entitlement exists.
  • Free allowance: the number of successful free exports is stored locally on the device.

Sharing and retention

Source metadata such as GPS coordinates is not intentionally copied to generated PDFs. Temporary source files, partial output, and abandoned files are subject to in-app cleanup. A PDF leaves the app only after you explicitly use Share or Save to Files.

Purchases

Apple may process purchase and App Store diagnostic information under Apple’s own terms. SizeProof does not receive your payment-card details.
